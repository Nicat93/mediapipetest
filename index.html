MediaPipe Games

Games you play with your body and hands. Tracking runs in your browser, and video never leaves your device.

🚲 Pose Rider Ride a bike down a country road. Steer by tilting your hands like handlebars. Body trackingAges 6+ 🏎️ Pose Driver Drive an open-top car, holding an imaginary steering wheel. First- and third-person views. Body trackingAges 6+ 🍳 Little Chef Cook pizza, soup, pancakes and more. Put the food in the pot with a wave of your hand or a tap. Hand trackingAges 3–5
Each game also works with touch, mouse or keyboard. Camera games need https (GitHub Pages) or localhost.
