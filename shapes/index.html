Copy My Hands

Make the same shape with your hands!

Grown-ups

A ghost hand shows a shape. Your child makes the same shape in front of the camera and holds it for a second. The closer the match, the more stars (up to 3). Fingers turn green when they match. Levels start with one hand and easy shapes (high five, fist, pointing), then add more fingers, tilted hands, two hands, and finally two different shapes at different heights.

Stand or sit so both hands fit in the picture, palms toward the camera. Hover a hand over 🏠 ⏭ 🔈 for a moment to press them. The video stays on this device.
