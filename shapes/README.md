# Copy My Hands

A ghost hand shows a shape; the child makes the same shape with their own hand in front of the camera
and holds it for a second. The closer the copy, the more stars (1–3). Fingers turn green when they
match, yellow when they're close and orange when they're wrong, and a meter shows the match in percent.

## Levels

All shapes are generated by a small 2D hand model (`js/poses.js`) from five finger curls, a thumb
direction, a finger spread, a tilt and a position. Nothing is hand-drawn, so new shapes are one line each.

| Levels | What changes | Pass mark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–5 | One hand, easy shapes: high five, fist, pointing finger, peace, thumbs up | 72% |
| 6–12 | One hand, more fingers to think about: three, bunny ears, phone, letter L, four, tiger claw, little finger | 74% |
| 13–17 | One hand, tilted 30–60° | 74% |
| 18–22 | Two hands, the same shape | 74% |
| 23–28 | Two hands, different shapes | 75% |
| 29+ | Two hands, different shapes, tilted, one high and one low (endless, generated from the level number) | 76% |

Progress (the next level) is saved in the browser. **Start again** on the start screen goes back to level 1.

## How the match works

- For each finger the game measures how straight it is (fingertip distance from the wrist compared with
  the knuckle's), and for the thumb how far its tip is from the middle of the palm. The same measurement
  runs on the generated target and on the tracked hand, so they compare like for like.
- Then it averages the fingers, pulled down hard by the worst one, so one wrong finger never passes.
- Either hand may copy a one-hand shape. With two hands, the hand on the left of the screen copies the left shape.
- Tilt counts a little on easy levels and more from level 13. Position counts only from level 29.
- The hand landmarks are smoothed with a One Euro filter, and the score is smoothed too, so the meter
  doesn't flicker.

Hover a hand over 🏠 (home), ⏭ (skip) or 🔈 (say it again) for 1.5 s to press them.
