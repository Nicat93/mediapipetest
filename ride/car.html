Pose Driver

Drive down the country road, steering with an imaginary wheel. The camera tracks your body with MediaPipe, right in the browser.

  1. Stand back so your head, shoulders and hands are in view.
  2. Hold both hands out in front of you as if gripping a steering wheel at 10 and 2.
  3. Turn the wheel: right hand down = right, left hand down = left.
  4. Dodge cones, rocks and barriers. Grab the coins!

No webcam? Pick “Phone (Wi‑Fi)” as the camera and scan the code with your phone; video goes straight from phone to computer and nothing is uploaded. Keyboard: ← → or A / D. Touch: hold the left or right side of the screen. Press V or the FPV button for a first-person view, ⚙ for steering settings.
